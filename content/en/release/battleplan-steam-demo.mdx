## Inside Battleplan: What You'd Be Testing

If a **Battleplan Steam demo** did exist, the systems below are what it would need to showcase. Understanding them now is the next best thing, because they tell you whether this game matches how you like to play.

Battleplan is not a click-fest RTS. You issue high-level orders, then watch your officers and companies carry them out autonomously. Battles are simulated second by second across missions that run anywhere from a week to a month of in-game time, and you can pause, play in real time, or fast-forward whenever you like.

The signature feature is plan drawing. You sketch multi-step, multi-unit orders directly onto the map — defence in depth, planned withdrawals, counterattacks, preparatory bombardments — then split divisions to grab several objectives at once. It's a system built for players who think in terms of operational art rather than unit micromanagement.

| Core system | How it works | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plan drawing | Draw multi-step, multi-unit orders on the map | Enables complex offensives without menu diving |
| Order of battle | Divisions modeled with HQs, regiments, battalions, companies | You customize your entire army structure |
| Chain of command | Hire and fire officers; they earn veterancy and quirks | Leadership choices carry long-term consequences |
| Real-world battlefields | Maps built from geographic data, with real elevation | Towns, roads, forests, and swamps sit where they actually are |
| Time controls | Pause, real time, or fast-forward | Play at whatever pace suits you |
| Logistics | Supply flows along terrain; cut lines, capture depots | Starving an enemy can win a fight without a shot |
| History layer | Geo-tagged historical photos and video | Learn the real events behind each battlefield |

Units span infantry, tanks, engineers, anti-tank guns, artillery, and scouts, and you can combine divisions into corps or split them down to companies. Requisition and upgrades run throughout the campaign, so the army you finish with looks nothing like the one you started with.
